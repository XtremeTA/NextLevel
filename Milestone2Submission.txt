Q1: What is the best part of our project?
A1: I think the best part is how every enemy causes a domino effect of more bricks being broken, which ends up
spawning more enemies and the cycle continues. This element feels very "arcade-y" and we are really happy with
how it turned out!

Q2: Is there anything in the game that doesn't feel done? 
A2: We would have loved to polish our art even further, adding more detailed enemy sprites and animations. 
However, we think, given the allotted time, we are really happy with the outcome overall.

Q3: What small tweaks can you make that will have the biggest overall impact on polish and game feel?
A3: After playtesting we plan on balancing our game heavily depending on feedback. We found it difficult to
properly balance our game. At first it was too easy, then much too difficult; we feel we have reached the sweet
spot but plan on pushing that even further once feedback is given.

Q4: Does anything need to be removed from the game?
A4: Currently we don't think anything needs to be removed from the game. However, this is very dependent on player
feedback that we will receive tomorrow.
